# What's new

## 0.3.8

- The brief and triage now set older open conversations aside as 'decide or archive' and never draft replies to them. Replies are never drafted twice on one thread. Meeting prep notes invites you have not accepted and shows local time when you travel. Your settings folder keeps earlier versions in an archive folder, and anything you ask a routine to always do is kept across updates.


## 0.3.7

- The brief and triage now cover everything still waiting in your inbox, not just new mail. Every run saves what it said to your journal folder. Filing starts as soon as you approve the plan; labels only is a choice you can make during setup.


## 0.3.6

- Setup no longer offers a paragraph to paste into your Claude profile; Cowork knows about the assistant on its own.


## 0.3.5

- Wording: once you say yes to a routine, the assistant creates its scheduled task for it itself.


## 0.3.4

- Setup now creates your scheduled tasks itself and reads them back, at exactly the times you chose. It no longer guesses your role, keeps its closing short, leaves your other scheduled tasks alone, and tells you how to tidy older mail after the first week.


## 0.3.3

- The assistant only writes drafts and never sends email, and it tells you so during setup. Requests to support are prepared as a draft for you to send.


## 0.3.2

- Results arrive in the app only. The assistant never sends email on its own.

## 0.3.1

- Setup no longer asks for an email address up front; it only comes up if you choose email for a routine.

## 0.3.0

- The assistant now lives in Cowork, on your phone, the web and the desktop app.
- It sets up its own scheduled tasks for you to confirm with one tap; nothing to paste.
- Results arrive as a notification in the app. Email is optional.
- It asks in plain sentences; answer however you like.

## 0.2.0

- Clearer, more careful behaviour across every part of the assistant: it checks before it concludes, asks one thing at a time, never sends or moves anything without your say-so, and speaks plainly.
- If you correct it, it writes the correction down and keeps to it.

## 0.1.4

- The inbox review always looks at the last 90 days and tells you how many messages it studied before proposing anything.

## 0.1.3

- Setup goes straight from your writing voice into the inbox review; no task or contact questions up front.
- Your key contacts are suggested from who you actually write to and meet, for you to confirm.
- Questions with options are numbered so you can answer with a single number.

## 0.1.2

- Internal: checks that updates reach your account automatically.

## 0.1.1

- Setup now starts reliably when you say "set up my personal assistant" in a normal chat.

## 0.1.0

First release of your Beyond Personal Assistant.

- Set up in a short conversation: say "set me up".
- Inbox organiser: learns how your inbox works, agrees a filing plan with you, then keeps it tidy.
- Morning brief by email, if you switch it on.
- Meeting prep for tomorrow by email, if you switch it on.
- Weekly inbox digest so you can correct anything it filed.
- Replies drafted in your voice, never sent without you.
- Quick capture of tasks, notes and people.
- Support check and a guided way to request new features.
