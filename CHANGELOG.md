## 0.1.0

First release of your Beyond Personal Assistant.

- Set up in a short conversation: say "set me up".
- Inbox organiser: learns how your inbox works, agrees a filing plan with you, then keeps it tidy.
- Morning brief by email, if you switch it on.
- Meeting prep for tomorrow by email, if you switch it on.
- Weekly inbox digest so you can correct anything it filed.
- Replies drafted in your voice, never sent without you.
- Quick capture of tasks, notes and people.
- Support check and a guided way to request new features.
